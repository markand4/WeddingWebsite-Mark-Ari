The Venue

Royal Palms Resort & Spa

5200 E. Camelback Road  ·  Phoenix, Arizona 85018

Nestled at the foot of Camelback Mountain, the Royal Palms Resort & Spa is one of the American Southwest's most beloved romantic destinations. Originally built in 1929 as the private winter estate of New York financier Delos Willard Cooke, the property was modeled after a Spanish Colonial villa and has never lost its timeless Mediterranean character.

Towering royal palms, fragrant citrus groves, cascading bougainvillea, and intimate garden courtyards create a lush, secluded atmosphere worlds away from the Arizona desert surrounding it. Hand-painted tiles, wrought-iron details, terracotta rooftops, and arched colonnades lend every moment an effortlessly old-world elegance — the perfect backdrop for a celebration of love.
