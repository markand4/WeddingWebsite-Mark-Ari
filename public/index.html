How It All Began

Our Story

We found each other the way most great things do — quietly, and without warning. It was sophomore year of college, brought together through mutual friends in Greek life. Neither of us said anything right away.

For months, we each carried a quiet crush, apparently assuming the other wasn't interested — each of us waiting, each of us wrong. Thankfully, our friends eventually intervened, confirming what everyone around us seemed to already know.

We made it official on November 24th, 2018, the night of the ASU vs. UofA game — a date Mark leaned on heavily in those early years when anniversaries came around. From the beginning, Ari was drawn to his humor and the genuine kindness behind it. Mark thought she was adorable and sweet. He still does.

In the years since, we've grown — as individuals and together. We've cheered each other through the highs and steadied each other through the harder moments in between. Along the way, we added three thoroughly spoiled pets to our family and bought our first home in 2022, building something that is undeniably, entirely ours.

Looking back on those early college days, neither of us could have predicted any of it. But standing here now, we think we can both say: it's exactly what we were hoping for.

Nestled at the foot of Camelback Mountain, the Royal Palms Resort & Spa is one of the American Southwest's most beloved romantic destinations. Originally built in 1929 as the private winter estate of New York financier Delos Willard Cooke, the property was modeled after a Spanish Colonial villa and has never lost its timeless Mediterranean character.

Towering royal palms, fragrant citrus groves, cascading bougainvillea, and intimate garden courtyards create a lush, secluded atmosphere worlds away from the Arizona desert surrounding it. Hand-painted tiles, wrought-iron details, terracotta rooftops, and arched colonnades lend every moment an effortlessly old-world elegance — the perfect backdrop for a celebration of love.

Explore the Valley of the Sun

While You're Here

Camelback Mountain

Hiking · 0.2 miles from venue

The iconic summit right outside the resort. Echo Canyon Trail is a challenging 1.5-mile climb rewarded with panoramic views of the entire Valley. Go early — sunrise from the top is unforgettable.

Desert Botanical Garden

Nature · 10 minutes away

140 acres of stunning desert flora, including the world's largest collection of cacti. The garden glows at golden hour — an ideal stop for anyone wanting to experience the Sonoran Desert up close.

Old Town Scottsdale

Dining & Nightlife · 10 minutes away

Art galleries, boutique shops, rooftop bars, and acclaimed restaurants tucked into a walkable historic district. Grab dinner at Citizen Public House or drinks at Postino before the weekend festivities.

Sedona Red Rocks

Day Trip · 2 hours north

Otherworldly red sandstone formations, spiritual vortex sites, world-class spas, and some of the best hiking in the American Southwest. A perfect escape for the day before or after the wedding.

Grand Canyon South Rim

Day Trip · 3.5 hours north

One of the seven natural wonders of the world is just a scenic drive away. Walk the Rim Trail, watch the sunrise over the canyon, or arrange a mule ride into the gorge. A once-in-a-lifetime stop.

Biltmore Fashion Park

Shopping & Dining · 5 minutes away

An open-air luxury shopping destination steps from the Arizona Biltmore. Browse Saks Fifth Avenue, dine at True Food Kitchen, or simply enjoy a coffee in the shadow of Frank Lloyd Wright's masterpiece.
